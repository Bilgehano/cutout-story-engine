# /create

Read `references/storytelling.md` and `references/sound.md` (in this skill). Text and JSON only: no components, stills, audio, samples or renders. Never change shared voice defaults.

The creator may give as little as a few sentences, a novel summary, or just "next episode". Make every creative decision yourself, applying the storytelling playbook by default; ask only when two of their constraints conflict or something cannot be rendered. Never stop to ask what they want the hook, transitions, sound or pacing to be: that is this workflow's job.

**Live gate (also for revisions):** first do a read-only voice lookup through the ElevenLabs MCP tools. If unavailable or unauthorized, stop and send the creator to `/setup`; no REST, receipt or cached substitute. The model is `voiceModel` from `session -- summary`; for a new series verify `eleven_v4` or `eleven_v3` is available for account and language (`GET /v1/models`) and use it, else report the blocker.

1. **Open.** New: `npm run session -- new`. Revision: stay in the newest `sessions/episode-N`; never overwrite another. Continuity: `npm run session -- summary` (series, cast, sets, recent episodes; planned events are not canon until approved).
2. **Series.** If `summary` has no `series`, write `series.json` from the creator's premise or novel summary (playbook section 1): logline, genre, audience, tone, protagonist want, wound and look, world rules, an 8-12 beat `arc`, and the end-card text. Otherwise take the next unplayed `arc` beat, unless the creator asked for something else; update `arc` if this episode changes it.
3. **Story.** Put the creator's must-haves in `brief.md`. Sketch two tellings that differ in what the viewer learns first, pick the one with the stronger cold open, note why in one sentence, present only that one. Build it in the playbook's episode shape: cold open on the most extreme moment in close-up with a `title` card, the premise or stake within 10s, 3-5 escalating turns, a payoff and an open ending, an `endCard`. Record `openingHook`/`closingHook`.
4. **Assets** (only those used, including off-screen speakers).
   - Returning: `{"id":"Mara","source":"reuse"}` only.
   - New character: an exaggerated, instantly readable archetype (playbook section 7): silhouette, face, hair, palette, status markers, role (`design`, max 60 words), poses, all `outfits`/`conditions` the series will need, gesture intensity, a `voiceId` from the account catalog whose age, gender and language labels match the character (premade on free tier; verify supplied IDs and say when their labels do not match; no collisions) and a baseline `voiceSettings`.
   - New location: framing, floor line, layers, palette, lighting, occlusion, props (max 50 words). Plan every prop a scene acts on (door, phone, poster, chat) and every state it needs. Changed art means a new ID, or ask before `npm run session -- unlock <id>` and list it as `build` with its full definition.
5. **`plan.json`** per the project's `scripts/plan.schema.json` (shape: the project's `examples/episode-plan.json`). Scene lengths sum to the runtime. `beats`: one short sentence per visible or audible change. Only renderable actions; split scenes to change blocking, pose, shot or location; `x` is a canvas-width fraction.
   - Each scene has a `turn` (want, obstacle, change), a `transition` into it and a `perspective`; memories get a `label`, `flash` in and out, no ambience, and an explicit return to the present (playbook section 4).
   - Each sentence or change of intention is its own non-overlapping `atSec`/`durationSec` line with playable `intent`, 1-3 `tags` (no brackets) and clean `text`. Estimate slot lengths at about 14 characters per second; `produce` fits them to the recorded speech, so do not pad. Keep motive out of `text`. Omit `voiceSettings` to use the speaker baseline; override only when needed (stability within 0.25, similarity within 0.15; v3 stability 0, 0.5 or 1). `highlight:true` on the hook, the key reveal and the vow.
   - Write for the ear: numbers, dates, times and abbreviations as this speaker says them (`19:30` -> `halb acht` for a teenager, `neunzehn Uhr dreißig` for an announcement). No editorial dashes or ellipses; use separate slots and timed silence.
   - `ambience` is a steady bed or `null`. Every audible action has a timed `sfx` (source, surface, rhythm, distance, decay; `volume` if needed); words belong in `lines`, wordless vocals in `sfx`. Follow the sound "never" list. Thoughts get a visible eye or mood event. Move the camera on every scene longer than 6s.
6. **Check once**, then fix: each must-have has a scene; each cut adds knowledge or tension; lines sound like these people without names or tags; each reveal has a renderable image plus a line, sound or silence; sound-only and picture-only reading of every boundary is clear; no effect masks a key word; the playbook checklist passes.
7. `npm run session -- lock`, fix errors, then fix or justify every `warnings` entry (one sentence each in `brief.md`). Present the scenes in order as a table with time, picture, lines and sound; then the hook, the ending and the runtime. Mark anything you invented that the creator might want to change (names, chat messages, numbers on screen). Stop for the creator's verdict; `/video` means accepted.
